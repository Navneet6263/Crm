# Green Call CRM - Product Documentation

Green Call CRM is a web-based CRM platform designed to help businesses manage leads, customers, team workflows, follow-ups, reporting, and internal coordination from one system. It is built for companies that want better control over sales activity without depending on disconnected spreadsheets, chats, or manual records.

This document is meant to help content writers understand the product clearly so they can create website content, SEO pages, landing pages, brochures, and feature-focused business copy.

## Product Summary

Green Call CRM helps teams handle the full lead journey from inquiry to closure. A lead can be created, assigned, updated, filtered, exported, tracked by product, moved across workflow stages, and reviewed later through history and performance reports.

The product is not limited to lead entry only. It also supports company-level operations where one business can create its team, assign responsibilities, track work, and manage lead flow in an organized way.

## What Green Call CRM Helps Businesses Do

- Capture and manage leads in one place
- Track status, notes, assignments, and follow-ups
- Filter and export lead data based on business needs
- Manage multiple users under one company setup
- Assign leads to sales, legal, and finance teams
- Monitor user performance and reporting
- Maintain customer records after lead conversion
- Manage workflow documents and operational history

## Who It Is For

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Sales-focused companies
- Service businesses handling regular inquiries
- Businesses that need team-based lead assignment
- Companies that want one CRM for operations, tracking, and reporting
- Organizations that need post-sales workflow handling through legal and finance teams

## How the System Works

Green Call CRM works as a central platform where a company can create users, manage leads, assign ownership, review progress, and export filtered data when needed. Instead of keeping information in different places, the business can run lead operations from one CRM dashboard.

A typical flow can look like this:

1. A lead is added into the system.
2. The lead is assigned to a sales user or relevant team member.
3. Notes, updates, status changes, and follow-up actions are recorded.
4. If the lead is converted or reaches a successful stage such as close won, the workflow can move ahead.
5. The legal team can handle documents and legal stage processing.
6. After legal completion, the finance team can handle invoices, payment-related work, and final completion.
7. The full history remains available for tracking, filtering, and reporting.

## Main Product Features

### 1. Lead Management

Lead management is the core of Green Call CRM. Users can add leads manually and maintain all important business details in one record.

Lead records can include:

- Contact person details
- Company name
- Phone number and email
- Product interest
- Lead source
- Priority
- Status
- Notes and updates
- Assigned user
- Created by
- Date and activity history

This helps teams know who the lead belongs to, what product the lead is related to, what stage it is in, and what action has already been taken.

### 2. Lead Assignment and Team Ownership

The CRM supports lead assignment so businesses can clearly track which user is handling which lead. This is useful for accountability, follow-up management, and team reporting.

It helps answer practical business questions such as:

- Which lead is assigned to which employee
- Who created a lead
- Which user updated a lead
- Which team is currently handling the lead
- What notes were added during the process

### 3. Lead History and Advanced Filters

One of the strongest parts of Green Call CRM is lead history. This module helps users extract exactly the records they need from stored lead data.

Lead history can be filtered using multiple parameters such as:

- Product
- Date
- Date range
- Status
- Lead source
- Priority
- Created by
- Assigned to
- Workflow stage
- Notes text
- Notes name
- Search by lead name or company details

This is especially useful when teams want to check a specific set of leads and download only relevant records.

Examples:

- Show all leads for a specific product
- Check leads created in a date range
- View leads assigned to a particular employee
- Find leads updated by a specific person in notes
- Extract leads for one source only
- Get filtered leads for reporting or review

### 4. Notes, Updates, and Activity Tracking

When users update leads, the CRM keeps a record through notes and history. This makes the system useful not just for storage, but for operational tracking.

This helps businesses review:

- What was updated on the lead
- Who added the note
- Which user worked on it
- What action was taken earlier
- What happened before the lead moved forward

For teams, this becomes important when multiple users handle the same lead over time.

### 5. Export and Sheet-Based Reporting

Green Call CRM supports exporting filtered lead data in sheet-friendly formats. This is useful for business reviews, management reporting, operations tracking, and offline sharing.

Teams can export data after applying filters so the output contains only relevant records. This is useful for:

- Product-wise reports
- User-wise lead reports
- Date-wise lead extraction
- Notes-based analysis
- Assignment-based reporting
- Daily or weekly operational review

The platform is designed in a way that businesses do not have to download all records every time. They can first filter, then export.

### 6. Workflow After Lead Closure

Green Call CRM also supports workflow-based operations after the sales side is completed.

A simple example:

- Sales team handles the lead first
- When the lead reaches a successful stage such as close won, the process can move forward
- The legal team can manage legal-stage work and documents
- After legal completion, the finance team can take over for invoice or payment-related steps
- Once finance work is completed, the lead or workflow can move to final completion

This makes the CRM more useful for businesses that do not stop at lead conversion and need post-sales operational handling.

### 7. Legal and Finance Team Use

The platform can be used by different teams with different roles.

Legal team usage can include:

- Reviewing workflow-stage leads
- Managing legal documents
- Handling agreement-related steps
- Tracking work history
- Updating stage progress

Finance team usage can include:

- Reviewing finance-stage leads
- Handling invoice-related details
- Tracking payment status
- Completing final workflow actions
- Maintaining final business processing records

This team-based structure makes the CRM useful beyond simple sales tracking.

### 8. Customer Management

After lead conversion, the business can continue the journey through customer management. This helps keep customer information and related business records organized.

This is useful for:

- Tracking converted customers
- Maintaining company and contact records
- Reviewing previous interactions
- Handling post-conversion business data

### 9. User Performance and Reporting

Green Call CRM includes reporting views that help management understand team performance and lead handling activity.

This supports questions like:

- How many leads did a user handle
- Which user received which leads
- Which product is generating more lead activity
- Which time period had more lead creation
- Which source is performing better

This makes it easier for admins and managers to review productivity without manually checking each record.

### 10. Company Setup and Team Management

Green Call CRM is not only for individual users. A company can be created and then operated as one organized system.

This means:

- A business can create its company profile
- Users can be added under the same company
- Roles can be managed based on access
- Leads can stay within the company structure
- One team can manage all operations from the same platform

In simple terms, one company setup can control the overall CRM usage for its team.

### 11. Product-Based Lead Tracking

If the business deals with multiple products or services, Green Call CRM can help track which leads belong to which product.

This is useful for:

- Product-wise lead review
- Product-wise export
- Product-wise reporting
- Comparing product demand
- Checking which team is handling which product leads

### 12. Search and Operational Visibility

The CRM includes search and filtering features so teams can find data faster instead of manually checking long lists.

This improves:

- Daily lead review
- Team handover
- Management reporting
- Internal tracking
- Operational clarity
